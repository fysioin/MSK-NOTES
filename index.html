How the ratings work

Repetition score R counts how many syllabus units a topic appears in across the papers (1–4). Blueprint score B is the number of questions the university topic-wise weightage allots it (each paper carries 10 questions). Combined C = R + B converts to stars: C≥5 → five stars, C=4 → four, C=3 → three, C=2 → two. Every paper page carries its own priority table. All notes were verified by independent review passes before publication; sources and verification notes appear at the end of each paper page.
