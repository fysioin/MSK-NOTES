## 3. FINAL correction list (one-line instructions)

### Paper II (Agent A)

1. **Body composition:** where the notes rank BIA above hydrodensitometry, correct to the hierarchy DEXA/multicomponent > hydrodensitometry > BIA ≈ skinfolds.
2. **Scapulohumeral rhythm:** where the notes apply the 2:1 ratio to the first 90°, correct to: the 2:1 glenohumeral:scapular ratio applies across the total arc of elevation (0–180°), the first ~30° being a variable "setting phase."
3. **TMJ arthrokinematics:** where the notes imply early translation or central-deviation tolerance, correct to: normal opening begins with pure rotation (~20–25 mm interincisal) followed by translation to 35–55 mm with the midline staying central; any deviation on opening is a dysfunction sign (e.g., disc displacement with reduction).
4. **Amputee energy cost (modify, don't just delete):** where the notes say an AK amputee uses "double the oxygen," replace with: oxygen cost per metre increases by roughly 40–100% depending on level and cause (Waters et al. 1976: traumatic TF ~+41%, vascular TF ~+102%, traumatic TT ~+12%).
5. **Femoral anteversion:** where the notes state normal adult anteversion as 12–15°, correct to: normal adult average ~10–12° (range 8–15°); 12–15° is the upper end.
6. **Gait maturation (annotate, do not delete):** keep "~7 years for adult-pattern gait" but add: most kinematic parameters are adult-like by 3–4 years, with full maturation by ~7–8 years (Sutherland).
7. **Older-adult base of support (annotate):** keep "wider base in older adults" only if labelled a textbook convention with mixed supporting evidence.
8. **"Four tissue systems" (no change):** treat as a syllabus convention, not a fact to correct — leave the draft wording alone.

### Paper III (Agent B)

9. **K-levels:** where the notes assign household ambulation to K2, correct to: K1 = household ambulator, K2 = limited community ambulator (low-level barriers), K3 = community ambulator with variable cadence, K4 = exceeds basic community demands (Medicare FCL).
10. **"Sinister Six":** relabel as informal teaching shorthand (content accurate); do not present as a Magee/Dutton term.
11. **PSFS:** where the notes call PSFS "the most responsive" outcome measure, correct to: PSFS is highly responsive, typically more so than generic or region-specific measures.

### Paper IV (Agent C)

12. **Mulligan "POD":** delete the fabricated acronym wherever it appears; replace with the genuine Mulligan terms PILL (Pain-free, Instant result, Long-Lasting) and CROCKS, and state the underlying theory as positional faults; retain the MWM description as correct.
13. **TENS evidence:** where the notes claim evidence for TENS is strong, correct to: Cochrane 2019 overview found very-low-quality evidence — a benefit for chronic pain cannot be confirmed or excluded; retain the shockwave-for-tendinopathy and NMES-as-adjunct statements.
14. **CBT misfiling:** move dry needling, cupping and IASTM out of the CBT section into adjuvant/passive physical interventions.
15. **Core stability:** where the notes claim segmental stabilisation is superior for LBP, correct to: comparable to general exercise, with at most a modest short-term pain advantage.
16. **WHO analgesic ladder (footnote only):** keep the classic 3-step ladder as the standard exam answer and add a footnote: WHO 2018 cancer-pain guidance no longer prescribes the ladder, describing it as only a general guide.
17. **Maitland grades:** add Grade V (small-amplitude, high-velocity thrust/manipulation at end of range) to the Maitland I–IV listing.
18. **Typo:** correct "Reverse-ibility" to "Reversibility."
19. **Coined mnemonics:** label OVER, TMDP, FAR-VP and HIP as coined (non-textbook) study aids — acceptable to keep, not citable.
