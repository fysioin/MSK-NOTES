# Verification Report — RGUHS MPT Paper I Study Notes (Draft)

Scope: all factual claims checked against World Physiotherapy/APTA documents, ICMR publications, the NCAHP Act 2021, the MoHFW Model Curriculum Handbook, and standard references (Hulley; McArdle, Katch & Katch; standard neurophysiology). Corrections only; no rewrite.

## 1. CORRECTED FACTS

1. **Section 2 — ICMR RIPE policy year is 2019, not 2020.** The ICMR Policy on Research Integrity and Publication Ethics (RIPE) was released in 2019 by the ICMR Bioethics Unit, NCDIR Bengaluru (institutional copy dated 30.07.2019; contemporaneous press reporting confirms release in August 2019). Content attribution (plagiarism, authorship, conflict of interest, ICMJE/COPE compliance) is correct. (Sources: icmr.gov.in-hosted RIPE policy PDF; ThePrint, 3 Sep 2019.)
   - Related, not an error: the ICMR National Ethical Guidelines 2017 year is correct and remains the current parent document; ICMR has since issued addenda in Feb 2024 (systematic reviews/meta-analyses) and Mar 2025 (integrative medicine research). (Source: ICMR Bioethics Unit guidelines listing, ethics.ncdirindia.org.)

2. **Section 2 — The seven "core values" are attributed to the wrong body.** Accountability, altruism, compassion/caring, excellence, integrity, professional duty and social responsibility come from the APTA Code of Ethics for the Physical Therapist / Core Values for the Physical Therapist and Physical Therapist Assistant (HOD P05-07-19-19, amended through 2021), not from World Physiotherapy. World Physiotherapy's benchmark is a different, correctly named pair: the policy statement "Standards of physical therapist practice" (re-approved 2019; latest revision 2023) and the guideline "Standards of physical therapy practice" (2011). Keep the two attributions distinct.

## Verified correct (high-yield items most often mis-taught)

- NCAHP Act 2021: the draft's wording ("practises independently or as part of a multidisciplinary team; minimum qualification baccalaureate degree") matches the Act's Schedule description of the Physiotherapy Professional; physiotherapists are regulated as healthcare professionals under the Act.
- MoHFW Model Curriculum Handbook, Physiotherapy, Allied Health Section 2015-16: real document, exact title/date as given.
- Exercise physiology: carbohydrate 4 kcal/g, protein 4 kcal/g, fat 9 kcal/g; BMR largest component of daily energy expenditure; ATP-PC ~0-10 s, anaerobic glycolysis up to ~2 min; neural adaptation precedes hypertrophy.
- Electrophysiology: nerve RMP ~ -70 mV, skeletal muscle ~ -90 mV; A-alpha (motor/proprioception), A-beta (touch), A-gamma (muscle spindle efferents), A-delta (fast pain/temperature), B (preganglionic autonomic), C (slow pain/postganglionic), conduction velocity descending A to C; NMJ sequence AP -> Ca2+ influx -> ACh release -> end-plate potential; rheobase (minimum intensity at long duration), chronaxie (duration at twice rheobase); denervated muscle requires millisecond-duration pulses, not microseconds.
- Biostatistics: 68-95-99.7 rule; odds ratio for case-control, relative risk for cohort; alpha 0.05, power = 1 - beta (80%); correct parametric/non-parametric pairings (Mann-Whitney U, Wilcoxon signed-rank, Kruskal-Wallis); Pearson r vs Spearman rho.
- Research methodology: Sackett's EBP definition; FINER; PICO(T); PEDro as physiotherapy-specific database; PROSPERO/PRISMA/I-squared; IMRAD.
- Pedagogy/management: Miller's pyramid (Knows -> Knows how -> Shows how -> Does); Dreyfus five stages (novice -> advanced beginner -> competent -> proficient -> expert); revised Bloom's cognitive levels (remember -> create); Knowles' andragogy assumptions; Fayol's five functions and the PODCC mnemonic; Taylor (scientific management) and Mayo (Hawthorne studies) attributions.
- Mnemonics: "A-B-N-J" (Autonomy, Beneficence, Non-maleficence, Justice) and "PODCC" (Planning, Organising, Directing/Commanding, Coordinating, Controlling) both map correctly and are functional.

## 2. UNVERIFIABLE / FLAGGED CLAIMS

- Section 1 competency list (patient care, medical/clinical knowledge, practice-based learning, interpersonal/communication skills, professionalism, systems-based practice): verbatim the ACGME six-competency framework from graduate medical education. Presented unattributed under physiotherapy competencies, it cannot be tied to a World Physiotherapy or Indian physiotherapy source — attribute it or label it a borrowed framework.
- Section 6 "learning styles": matching instruction to learning styles has no empirical support and is widely classed as a neuromyth. Safe as a topic heading; misleading if taught as evidence-based practice.
- "Sackett 5 A's": the five-step model is standard, but the "5 A's" phrasing originates in EBP-implementation literature rather than Sackett's own writing — minor attribution looseness, not an error.

## 3. VERDICT

Factually sound overall — only two real corrections needed (RIPE is 2019, not 2020; the seven core values belong to APTA, not World Physiotherapy) plus attribution cleanup on the ACGME competency list.
